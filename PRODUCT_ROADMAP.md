# Product Roadmap - BITS MBA Analytics Prep

This document outlines planned features and improvements for future versions of the platform.

## Version 2.0 - Content Management & Robustness

### 🛠 Bulk Upload Enhancements
- [ ] **Dry Run Mode**: A pre-upload validation step that shows exactly how many questions will be added, skipped (duplicates), or errored before committing to the database.
- [ ] **Question Review Queue**: Implement a "Draft" status for bulk-uploaded questions, allowing admins to verify quality and formatting in the UI before they go live for students.
- [ ] **Conflict Resolution**: Option to "Overwrite" existing questions if the CSV contains updated versions of existing content.

### 🏢 Company Management
- [ ] **Automated Logo Verification**: Integration with a search-first logo strategy to ensure high-quality, up-to-date branding.
- [ ] **Sector-based Filtering**: Enhanced filtering for companies based on industry domains (e.g., FinTech, Healthcare, Consulting).

### 🤖 AI Assistant (V2)
- [ ] **Context-Aware Interview Simulation**: AI can simulate a mock interview based on specific company questions.
- [ ] **Personalized Prep Path**: Suggestions based on the user's focus areas.
